Who We Are

Vodi group is renowned Nigerian fashion conglomerate founded by Seyi vodi in 2001 initially started as a small tailoring business called testimony fabrics, It was later rebranded to Vodi Group in 2003. The company has grown in to a multifaceted fashion empire with several subsidiaries, including Vodi Tailor, Vodi Clean, Vodi Training institute, vodi Debo and Vodi Textile

Seyi Vodi, CEO Vodi Group

Seyi Vodi, whose real name is Seyi Adekunle, is a prominent Nigerian fashion entrepreneur, founder of the Vodi Group, and a celebrated tailor known for designing high-end customized menswear including suits, agbadas, and kaftans. His brand is recognized for blending traditional African design with modern styles and has a clientele of politicians, celebrities, and dignitaries worldwide.
